## Why

The deployed weekly coverage card repeats statuses, disclaimers, dates and evidence panels until its visual answer is lost. The user wants the compact, primarily visual presentation of the original timeline prototype.

## What Changes

- Show a single shared time axis with compact pool and account rows, availability bars, empty spans and reset markers.
- Keep one short takeaway and a shared legend visible. Put calibration evidence, excluded accounts and detailed assumptions behind one disclosure.
- In incomplete pool states, show independent account pace only through each current reset, explicitly distinguish it from a pooled forecast, and leave later time unknown.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `weekly-coverage`: Compact visual presentation and progressive disclosure.

## Impact

Dashboard React component, component and screenshot checks, translations and documentation. No backend, schema, routing or estimation changes.
